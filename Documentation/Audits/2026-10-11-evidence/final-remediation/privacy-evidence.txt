Contract/mechanism:
Dedicated serial userInitiated dispatch queue owns registration, removal, process/device lists, running-input/camera queries, proc_pidpath and Bundle metadata.
Live listener cancellation retains exactly the object/address/block/queue from its successful add.
Listener closures capture worker weakly; publications capture monitor weakly.
UI publications are submitted FIFO to main dispatch, with running/generation checks.
Stop clears visible flags/clients synchronously; background lifecycle remains ordered.
No polling, debounce, event coalescing, caching or feature removal.
Original other-process running-input detection, per-device camera detection and first-seen client identity/order retained.
